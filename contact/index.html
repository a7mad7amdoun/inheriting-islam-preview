About the surah & verse numbering

A surah is a chapter of the Qur’an; an ayah is a numbered verse. These totals follow the Kufan numbering used by Quran.com. Other traditional verse-counting systems exist.

وَقُل لِّعِبَادِي يَقُولُواْ ٱلَّتِي هِيَ أَحۡسَنُۚ

Translation of the meanings · Excerpt · 17:53

And tell My servants to say that which is best.

Saheeh International ↗ · via Al Quran Cloud

Opening clause of the verse

Meaning in brief · Original English paraphraseAllah instructs His servants to choose the best manner of speech.

A reflection from the house

A clear question, a careful correction or a kind introduction can make the next conversation easier.

Context & a step to carry with you

The full verse warns that Satan stirs discord between people and is an enemy to humankind. The instruction about speech is connected to that warning.

One step to carry with you

Name what you need and give enough context for a useful reply.

From the Prophetic teachings

Let your words carry good.

Prophet Muhammad ﷺ

صلى الله عليه وسلم

Sahih · Authentic narration

وَمَنْ كَانَ يُؤْمِنُ بِاللَّهِ وَالْيَوْمِ الآخِرِ فَلْيَقُلْ خَيْرًا أَوْ لِيَصْمُتْ

Sahih al-Bukhari 6018 ↗

Narrated by Abu Huraira

Excerpt · The closing teaching on speech

Meaning in brief · Our paraphraseBelief in Allah and the Last Day calls for speech that brings good, or the restraint to remain silent.

A step to carry into your day

A question, a correction, or a difficult experience can be shared with care. Be clear about what happened and what help you are asking for.

Context & source

The full narration also teaches care for neighbours and generous hospitality. Its teaching on speech belongs to that wider practice of treating people well.

The Arabic is from the linked narration. Our paraphrase and practical reflection are editorial writing, pending review; they are not part of the hadith text.

What happens next

From a draft to a conversation.

    Review, then send

    Preparing the email does not send it. Review the draft and press Send in your own email app. It goes to Hamza's inbox.

  2. 02

    A reply, usually within a day

    From a person, in shā' Allāh — with an answer, or an honest note that we are not the right people to ask.

  3. 03

    A call, only if it helps

    Some things are quicker to talk through, like hosting a cohort. If so, we will suggest a time on US hours. Nobody will chase you afterward.
