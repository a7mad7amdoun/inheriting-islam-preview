Learning the Qur’an, finding practical help, and sharing what benefits others: these are the purposes of Inheriting Islam. Hamza and Khadija founded this work to serve those needs, with teaching and care at its heart.

A letter from Hamza

The teaching
shapes the work.

“The deen is a mīrāth. We received it, we live it, and our whole job is to pass it on well.”

I taught Qur'an and led taraweeh before I ever wrote a line of code. That is not a line for a website — it is the order the two things happened in, and it decides how I build.

I have taught at Islah LA in Los Angeles, Midtown Mosque in Memphis, Masjid As-Sabur in Las Vegas, and Atlanta Masjid in Atlanta. Four cities, four communities, and the same thing needed in each of them.

Standing in front of a congregation teaches you something no design course does: you can tell within thirty seconds whether people are with you or being talked at. Software is the same, and most of it is talking at people.

This house is kept deliberately small, because I would rather do a small amount of work properly than take on more than we can carry well. When capacity is full, I say so.

My roots are in Los Angeles, and I am currently based in Morocco. Inheriting Islam is a US company serving English-speaking Muslims worldwide. Live Qur’an classes run on US time zones.

Hamza Abdul-Tawwaab Co-founder

Co-founder / Operations

Khadija Wade

Khadija co-founded Inheriting Islam with Hamza and runs operations: replying, keeping the work moving, and noticing when someone needs a little more help.

Her care keeps the practical side of the house connected to the people it serves.

Translation of the meanings · Complete verse · 49:13

O mankind, indeed We have created you from male and female and made you peoples and tribes that you may know one another. Indeed, the most noble of you in the sight of Allah is the most righteous of you. Indeed, Allah is Knowing and Acquainted.

Saheeh International ↗ · via Al Quran Cloud

Complete verse

Meaning in brief · Original English paraphraseHumanity shares an origin, and peoples and tribes are made to know one another. Honor before Allah rests in mindfulness of Him; Allah knows fully.

A reflection from the house

The house should welcome people across languages, backgrounds and stages of learning. Knowing someone begins with listening rather than making assumptions.

Context & a step to carry with you

The address is to humankind. It speaks of shared origin, difference and knowing one another, while placing honor before Allah in taqwa rather than ancestry.

One step to carry with you

Ask someone about their own experience without asking them to represent a whole community.

On knowledge that benefits others · Sahih Muslim 1631

A moment with the sources

Knowledge that remains useful.

Sahih Muslim 1631

From Prophet Muhammad ﷺ

Sahih · Authentic narration

أَوْ عِلْمٍ يُنْتَفَعُ بِهِ

English translation · Excerpt

or knowledge (by which people) benefit,

Translation published by Sunnah.com ↗

Meaning in brief · Original English paraphrase of the excerpt in context · Inheriting IslamThe Prophet Muhammad ﷺ includes knowledge that benefits others among three things whose benefit can continue after a person’s death.

A reflection from the houseOur responsibility is to make useful work, explain its limits, and keep it clear enough for someone else to learn from.

Read the source, context, and text notes

Arabic text: Hadith excerpt: the clause about beneficial knowledge.

Narrated by Abu Huraira.

The full narration also names continuing charity and a righteous child who prays for the deceased. It appears in the Book of Wills, in a chapter on reward after death. The excerpt is not a promise about any particular project.

The English above is a brief paraphrase, not a word-for-word translation. The reflection is our editorial writing.
