01.7 / Certificates & verification

A record of
what was learned.

Explore the three certificates for learning with Hamza, understand what each stage recognizes, and check the record of issued certificates.

Hamza’s original designs

Three stages. A clear record of learning.

Blank examples, shown as published by Inheriting Islam. These recognize learning demonstrated before a teacher; they are not an ijāzah or a license to transmit.

01 · Reading from the page

The Qāʿidah

Certificate of Completion

Recognizes reading unfamiliar text aloud before the teacher: joining the letters, forming their sounds, and reading correctly without prompting.

The first stage establishes independent reading before progressing to tajwīd.

02 · Reciting with care

Juz ʿAmma with Tajwīd

Certificate of Recitation

Recognizes reciting the whole of Juz ʿAmma aloud before the teacher, applying the rules of tajwīd throughout.

This stage builds on reading fluency before moving to memorization.

03 · Carrying it in memory

Juz ʿAmma from memory

Certificate of Memorization

Recognizes memorizing the whole of Juz ʿAmma and reciting it from memory before the teacher, with tajwīd maintained.

Its scope is Juz ʿAmma, rather than memorization of the entire Qur’an.

And do not pursue that of which you have no knowledge.

Saheeh International ↗ · via Al Quran Cloud

Opening clause of the verse

Meaning in brief · Original English paraphraseDo not pursue a claim without knowledge of it.

A reflection from the house

A learning record should say what was assessed and who assessed it. Read its stated scope before making a claim about someone’s qualification.

Context & a step to carry with you

The complete verse connects this instruction to accountability for hearing, sight and the heart. It is part of a passage of moral responsibilities.

One step to carry with you

Check the name, date and certificate scope; ask about anything unclear.

Editorial reflection · Review pending

How the number works

What is published, and what is not.

A certificate is only worth something if it can be checked. It is only decent if checking it does not turn our students into a public list.

The number

What it looks like

Printed at the bottom right of every certificate, in the form IQ-Q-1448-014 — the house, the stage (Q, T or Ḥ), the Hijri year, and the number within that year.

The register

What it will show

The number, which stage it certifies, and the month it was issued. Enough to confirm a certificate in your hand is real.

Never

What it will not show

Student names are not published. The register will confirm whether a certificate number was issued, its stage, and its issue month. It does not publicly verify the holder’s identity. Contact Hamza if you need to confirm the details on a certificate.

A certificate is not an ijāzah

Every one says so on its face. These certify what a student demonstrated to a teacher — read unseen text, applied tajwīd through a juz, recited it from memory. None of them confers a license to transmit, and none of them should ever be presented as one.

If a number does not check out

Write to hamza@inheritingislam.com with the number and we will tell you plainly. If somebody has put their own name on our work, we want to know before you do.
