    About the surah & verse numbering

    A surah is a chapter of the Qur’an; an ayah is a numbered verse. These totals follow the Kufan numbering used by Quran.com. Other traditional verse-counting systems exist.

    Read the surah guide ↗

    Translation of the meanings · Complete verse · 38:29

    [This is] a blessed Book which We have revealed to you, [O Muhammad], that they might reflect upon its verses and that those of understanding would be reminded.

    Saheeh International ↗ · via Al Quran Cloud

    Complete verse

    Meaning in brief · Original English paraphraseAllah describes the revealed Book as blessed, inviting reflection on its verses and remembrance by people of understanding.

    A reflection from the house

    A source deserves more than a passing glance. Read its words, consider the context, and keep your questions close.

    Context & a step to carry with you

    The Book in this verse is the revelation sent to the Prophet ﷺ. The verse connects its blessing with reflection and remembrance; it does not describe this website or its editorial writing.
