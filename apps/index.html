When someone dies

Life Is Temporary

Practical guidance for a family facing loss, with tools for those preparing ahead.

  • Follow the steps after a death and find funeral services.
  • Understand inheritance through the calculator.
  • Prepare a will draft to discuss with qualified advisers.
Live · freeOpen Life Is Temporary ↗

When a child arrives

First Adhān

A companion for your baby’s first days, bringing practical questions and source-linked guidance together.

  • Explore adhān, taḥnīk, and ʿaqīqah.
  • Plan the first week and read about the mother’s first forty days.
  • Browse Muslim names and their dictionary meanings.
Live · freeOpen First Adhān ↗

When you are traveling

A Room to Pray

Find a place to pray at the airport, with the details you need before reaching the terminal.

  • Check the terminal and which side of security.
  • Look for wuḍūʾ facilities, women’s spaces, and qibla information.
  • Follow official source links and see when details were checked.

About the surah & verse numbering

A surah is a chapter of the Qur’an; an ayah is a numbered verse. These totals follow the Kufan numbering used by Quran.com. Other traditional verse-counting systems exist.

Read the surah guide ↗

وَتَعَاوَنُواْ عَلَى ٱلۡبِرِّ وَٱلتَّقۡوَىٰۖ وَلَا تَعَاوَنُواْ عَلَى ٱلۡإِثۡمِ وَٱلۡعُدۡوَٰنِۚ

Excerpt: the two clauses about cooperation

Meaning in brief · Original English paraphraseHelp one another in goodness and mindfulness of Allah; do not help one another in wrongdoing or aggression.

A reflection from the house

These tools serve everyday needs. Their value depends on being clear, accurate and useful to the people who rely on them.

Context & a step to carry with you

The full verse concerns sacred rites, pilgrimage and the duty not to let hostility lead to transgression. Its call to cooperate in good is paired with a prohibition on cooperating in harm.

One step to carry with you

Share a tool with someone who needs it, and pass on any correction you notice.

Sahih · Authentic narration

وَاللَّهُ فِي عَوْنِ الْعَبْدِ مَا كَانَ الْعَبْدُ فِي عَوْنِ أَخِيهِ

Sahih Muslim 2699a ↗

Narrated by Abu Huraira

Excerpt · The clause about helping others

Meaning in brief · Our paraphraseAllah helps His servant while that servant helps his brother.

A step to carry into your day

Start with the person, then the tool. Help a traveller find a prayer space, or share a useful guide when a family actually needs it.

Context & source

Abu Huraira narrates a longer teaching about relieving hardship, seeking knowledge, and gathering to study the Qur’an. This excerpt concerns helping others; it makes no claim about the reward of a particular app.

The Arabic is from the linked narration. Our paraphrase and practical reflection are editorial writing, pending review; they are not part of the hadith text.

The care behind the work

Each app keeps its own identity.

Inheriting Islam is the publisher behind the work. The line “An Inheriting Islam app” connects the tools to the people responsible for them: Hamza and Khadija.

Access to what matters

Tools for learning and worship stay free or deliberately affordable. Obligatory worship is never placed behind a purchase.

A sustainable way to serve

Convenience features, sync, and institutional licenses can carry a price, helping support access for people who cannot afford to pay.

Respect for your privacy

No surveillance advertising or sale of user data. Where an app can work entirely on your device, it does.

Project history & status

A small team needs to give each tool enough attention. Three earlier projects are no longer being built; one playable project is set down, with Hamza intending to return to it. This record stays available to anyone who followed the work.
