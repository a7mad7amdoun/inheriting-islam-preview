About the surah & verse numbering

A surah is a chapter of the Qur’an; an ayah is a numbered verse. These totals follow the Kufan numbering used by Quran.com. Other traditional verse-counting systems exist.

Read the surah guide ↗

فَسۡـَٔلُوٓاْ أَهۡلَ ٱلذِّكۡرِ إِن كُنتُمۡ لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ

Translation of the meanings · Excerpt · 16:43

So ask the people of the message if you do not know.

Saheeh International ↗ · via Al Quran Cloud

Closing clause of the verse

Meaning in brief · Original English paraphraseThose who do not know are directed to ask people acquainted with the revelation.

A reflection from the house

A directory can point you toward a resource. A teacher can help you understand what to do when a resource leaves you uncertain.

Context & a step to carry with you

In this verse, the question concerns earlier messengers being human recipients of revelation. Asking a qualified teacher is our practical learning suggestion, distinct from the verse’s immediate context.

One step to carry with you
