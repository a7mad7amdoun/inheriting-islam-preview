Inheriting Islam · Editorial specimen

One identity. Two reading traditions.

Six complementary roles in each language: two related editorial voices, an artistic accent, everyday reading, and clear navigation. Sacred text stays complete and distinct from editorial writing.

01

Opening · Cormorant Garamond

An inheritance. A way of life.

ميراثٌ نحمله، وحياةٌ نعيشها.

Hamza’s original English wordmark and generous opening titles. Amiri provides the Arabic opening voice and complete sacred text.

02

Chapters · EB Garamond

A place to begin. A way to grow.

بدايةٌ مطمئنة، وخطواتٌ نحو العلم.

A calm editorial voice for section headings.

03

Details · EB Garamond

Care, in practice.

عنايةٌ تظهر في العمل.

Clear, composed titles for cards and smaller sections; never used for navigation.

04

Artistic accent · Cormorant Garamond

What we carry forward

ميراث

An occasional art caption, never an instruction or a religious quotation.

05

Everyday reading · Source Sans 3

Bring your questions and the experience you already have. Learning begins with someone listening.

أهلًا بأسئلتك وبما تحمله من تجربة. تبدأ رحلة التعلّم حين تجد من يُصغي إليك.

Explanations, captions, and ordinary reading. Comfortable measures and generous spacing.

06

Navigation · Manrope

القرآن · التطبيقات · البودكاست · عنّا

One stable face for menus, controls, forms, and moving through the house.

Qur’anic reading: marks and spacing

رَّبِّ زِدۡنِي عِلۡمٗا

Excerpt: the closing supplication of Taha 20:114. The registered Arabic is preserved exactly; text is never altered to fit a font.
