INHERITING ISLAM — ARTWORK COLLECTIONS 01 & 02
Prepared for Hamza, October 2026.

42 AI-generated proposals: 24 in the original zellige cut-paper collection and 18 in a new paper, stone and light collection.
Collection 02 combines layered paper with restrained brass and stone textures, including daylight and lamplight scenes.
These are review concepts, not a claim of Hamza's approval. Existing website assets have not been replaced.

FILES
The originals folder contains the native PNG outputs. No artificial resolution increase is applied.
The gallery lists each file's actual dimensions. JPEG previews are for browsing; use the original PNG for production.
catalog.json maps artwork numbers to titles, suggested uses, and filenames.
generation-prompts.txt records the style brief and scene prompts. Generated with the built-in image-generation tool.

USE
Designed as text-free illustrations for website sections and social posts. Add approved typography and captions separately.
Keep the main subject complete when cropping. Check the crop for each social platform rather than stretching the image.
These artworks do not replace the original brand logo, app symbols, portrait, book covers, or issued certificates.
The certificate illustration is a blank conceptual sheet, not a real credential. Unlettered books do not reproduce Quran text.
Review geometric detail and brand fit at full size before final use. The gallery does not certify religious meaning or attach religious claims to these illustrations.
